# PRODUCT REQUIREMENTS DOCUMENT (PRD)

## Project Name: CampusBite – Campus Food Ordering System

**Course:** BU-209 Backend Engineering  
**Assignment:** Week 4  
**Version:** 1.0

## 1. Project Overview

CampusBite is a web-based food ordering platform designed to help university students order food from campus cafeterias without spending unnecessary time waiting in queues.

Students can view available meals, check prices, place orders, and track their orders until they are ready for collection.

## 2. Problem Statement

Students often experience long queues when purchasing food on campus, especially during lunch breaks and busy periods. This wastes valuable time that could be used for academic activities.

Additionally, students sometimes have to visit cafeterias physically to check available meals and prices. Food vendors may also struggle to manage multiple orders efficiently.

CampusBite aims to solve these problems by providing an online food ordering platform.

## 3. Research and Findings

The team will investigate the problem through:

- Interviews with students.
- Interviews with cafeteria vendors.
- Observation of cafeteria activities.
- Questionnaires about students' food ordering experiences.

The research will focus on waiting times, ordering difficulties, food availability, and vendor challenges.

*Actual research findings will be added after collecting responses.*

## 4. Target Users

The system will have three main users:

- **Students:** Browse meals, place orders, and track orders.
- **Food Vendors:** Manage menus, receive orders, and update order statuses.
- **Administrators:** Manage users, vendors, and platform activities.


## 5. Proposed Solution

CampusBite will provide a centralized platform that connects students with campus food vendors.

Students can order meals online before arriving at the cafeteria, while vendors can manage incoming orders through a dashboard.

## 6. Product Goals

- Reduce unnecessary waiting time.
- Make food menus and prices accessible online.
- Improve communication between students and vendors.
- Make order management easier for vendors.
- Improve the campus food purchasing experience.

 ## 7. Minimum Viable Product (MVP)

The first version will include:

- User registration and authentication.
- Food menu management.
- Food ordering.
- Vendor order management.
- Basic order tracking.

Features such as online payments, delivery services, ratings, and loyalty programs will be considered for future versions.

 ## 8. Conclusion

CampusBite aims to improve the food ordering experience on university campuses by reducing unnecessary queues and making food ordering more convenient.

The initial version will focus on essential ordering and management features, with future improvements based on feedback from students and vendors.

The system will be developed based on actual research to ensure it addresses a genuine campus problem.
